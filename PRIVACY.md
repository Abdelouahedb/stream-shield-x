# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 November 2026_

Stream Shield for X does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## What the extension accesses

- **Pages on x.com and twitter.com.** The extension reads these pages inside your browser to find your own name, @handle, profile photo, notification counts and message previews, so it can cover them. This happens only on your device. Nothing it reads is saved or sent anywhere.
- **Your settings.** Your choices (shield on or off, cover style, what to cover, an optional fallback @handle) are saved with `chrome.storage.local` in your browser profile. They never leave your device.

## What the extension does not do

- It makes no network requests.
- It has no analytics, tracking, ads or remote code.
- It does not run on any site other than x.com and twitter.com.

## Removing your data

Removing the extension deletes its saved settings.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue at https://github.com/Abdelouahedb/stream-shield-x/issues.
